General information

What services does Blissful Senja offer?+

We provide professional mobile spa and massage services delivered directly to your villa, hotel, or home anywhere in Bali. Our menu includes Traditional Balinese Massage, Deep Tissue Massage, Reflexology, Foot Massage, Body Scrubs (Lulur), and beauty treatments like Manicures, Pedicures, and Hair Braiding.

What areas in Bali do you cover?+

Our therapists travel across the entire island. Whether you are staying in Seminyak, Canggu, Uluwatu, Ubud, Kuta, Sanur, or Nusa Dua, we bring the spa experience to your doorstep.

Are your therapists certified?+

Yes. All Blissful Senja therapists are professionally certified and have extensive experience in authentic Balinese healing techniques and therapeutic care to ensure you receive a safe and high-quality treatment.

What are your operating hours?+

We operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Whether you need a morning refresh or a late-night relaxation session after a long flight, our team is available around the clock.

Booking & SOP

How do I book a mobile massage session?+

Booking is simple. You can fill out the booking form on our website or contact us directly via WhatsApp for a faster response. We recommend booking at least 1–2 hours in advance to ensure therapist availability.

Do I need to provide any equipment?+

No. Our therapists arrive fully prepared with everything needed for your session, including massage oils and clean towels. You only need to provide a comfortable space (such as a bed or a quiet area in your villa) for the treatment.

Can I choose the gender of my therapist?+

Absolutely. You can specify your preference for a female or male therapist during the booking process, and we will do our best to accommodate your request based on availability.

Is there an extra fee for transportation?+

No, we believe in transparent pricing. Our listed prices are all-inclusive, meaning there are no hidden transport fees for locations across Bali.

Health & safety

What should I do if I have a health condition or injury?+

Please inform our team or your therapist before the session starts. Whether it is a recent injury, pregnancy, or specific muscle tension, our therapists can customize the pressure and techniques to suit your needs safely.

Is mobile massage safe for children or the elderly?+

Yes, we offer gentle treatments suitable for all ages. For children, we recommend a lighter touch and shorter duration, while for the elderly, we focus on improving circulation and mobility with care.

Choosing the right massage

Which massage is best after a long flight?+

We recommend the Balinese Traditional Massage. Long-haul flights cause fluid retention, poor circulation and muscle stiffness — especially in the legs, lower back and shoulders. The long flowing strokes and acupressure of a Balinese massage directly address these, restoring circulation and helping your body adjust to a new time zone faster.

Which massage helps most with jet lag?+

The Balinese Traditional Massage is the best choice for jet lag. It stimulates blood flow, eases the muscle tension caused by sitting in a pressurised cabin for hours, and promotes deep sleep — which is the single most effective remedy for jet lag. A 90-minute session in the evening after arrival works particularly well.

Which massage is best after surfing?+

Deep Tissue Massage. Surfing works the shoulders, rotator cuffs, lower back and hip flexors intensely. Our deep tissue technique uses targeted, firm pressure to release the specific muscle groups engaged during surfing — reducing next-day soreness and improving recovery speed.

Which massage is best after hiking — such as Mount Batur?+

Deep Tissue or Balinese Traditional, depending on your preference. For intense soreness and tight quads and calves after a demanding hike, Deep Tissue delivers faster relief. If you prefer something restorative and relaxing rather than firm, the Balinese Traditional is a gentler but equally effective option.

Which massage is best for tired, sore feet?+

Reflexology is specifically designed for this. It applies precise pressure to nerve points on the feet that correspond to different systems throughout the body — improving circulation, reducing swelling and relieving the localised soreness that comes from a full day of walking, beach trips or exploring Bali's markets and temples.

Which massage is best for office workers or digital nomads?+

The Balinese Traditional Massage addresses the most common issues for desk-based workers — tight neck and shoulders, lower back stiffness and eye strain headaches. If your symptoms are more severe (chronic neck pain or tension headaches from screen time), request firm pressure or upgrade to Deep Tissue. We can also combine with Facial Acupressure to target jaw and forehead tension.

Can I shower after a massage?+

Yes, but we suggest waiting at least 30–60 minutes. The oils used during your session continue to absorb and benefit the skin after the massage ends. A warm (not hot) shower afterward is perfectly fine.

During your Bali holiday

Can I book a massage after a Mount Batur hike?+

Yes, and we highly recommend it. The hike engages your quads, calves, glutes and lower back intensely — a Deep Tissue or Balinese massage the same evening or the following morning accelerates recovery significantly. Many guests book in advance for the evening after their hike.

Can I book a massage after a Nusa Penida day trip?+

Absolutely. The Nusa Penida trip typically involves a fast boat, steep cliff paths and a great deal of walking. Most guests return with sore legs, a stiff back and tired feet. A Reflexology or Balinese massage upon returning to Bali is the perfect way to recover before your next adventure.

Can I book a massage after yoga or a retreat?+

Yes. Massage and yoga complement each other well. A Balinese Traditional or Reflexology session after yoga deepens the relaxation response, helps release any residual muscle tension and enhances the meditative benefits of your practice.

Can I book a massage for a villa party or group?+

Yes. We specialise in group bookings. We can send 2–5 therapists simultaneously to your villa for birthday celebrations, bachelorette parties, retreat programmes or family gatherings. We recommend booking group sessions at least 24 hours in advance. Message us on WhatsApp for a custom quote.

Can I book a massage for my honeymoon or anniversary?+

Our Couple Massage is one of our most popular bookings for exactly this occasion. Two therapists arrive together and deliver a perfectly synchronised session — an intimate, private experience in your villa that no restaurant or spa outing can replicate.

Can I book a massage as a birthday surprise for someone?+

Yes. Many guests book as a gift for a partner or friend. Simply let us know it is a surprise in the notes field of your booking and we will coordinate the arrival time and any special details with you privately.

What are good rainy day activities in Bali?+

Bali's rain — especially during wet season — can catch visitors off-guard. A mobile massage is one of the best rainy day activities because it comes to you and turns a frustrated afternoon into a genuinely restorative one. Pair it with a good book, a bath and a room service meal for the perfect Bali staycation day.
