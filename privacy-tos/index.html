Privacy policy

How Blissful Senja collects, uses and protects your personal information — including our use of WhatsApp and Meta's messaging tools.

Last updated: 17 July 2026

1. Who We Are

Blissful Senja ("we", "us", "our") is a mobile spa and massage service operating across Bali, Indonesia. We deliver professional massage and beauty treatments to clients at their villa, hotel or home.

We can be contacted at:

  • WhatsApp: +62 823 1269 3971
  • Website: blissfulsenja.com
  • Email: hello@blissfulsenja.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you interact with us directly. This includes:

  • Contact details — your name, email address and WhatsApp or phone number
  • Booking details — your location in Bali, preferred date and time, service requested and session duration
  • Health information — any relevant health conditions, injuries or preferences you share with us to customise your treatment (provided voluntarily)
  • Payment information — we accept cash only; we do not process or store any card or bank data
  • Communications — messages you send us via WhatsApp, our booking form or email
  • Technical data — basic website usage data collected via Google Analytics (see Section 7)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data solely to:

  • Confirm and manage your booking
  • Coordinate therapist dispatch to your location
  • Contact you regarding your appointment via WhatsApp or email
  • Respond to enquiries and provide customer support
  • Improve our services based on general feedback

We do not use your data for unsolicited marketing, and we do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to any third party.

4. WhatsApp & Meta Chatbot — Specific Policy

Important notice for clients who contact us via WhatsApp: Blissful Senja uses WhatsApp Business (operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.) as our primary booking and communication channel. By messaging us on WhatsApp, you acknowledge that your messages are subject to Meta's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service in addition to this policy.

What this means for you:

  • Messages sent to our WhatsApp number are transmitted via Meta's infrastructure and subject to Meta's data practices. You can review Meta's Privacy Policy at whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Blissful Senja may use WhatsApp Business features including automated greeting messages, quick replies and away messages. These are operated by us, not by a third-party AI system.
  • If Blissful Senja uses Meta's AI-powered chatbot features (including Meta AI or any automated response tools within WhatsApp Business), we will clearly disclose this. At present, our WhatsApp responses are handled by our human team.
  • We do not use your WhatsApp conversation data for advertising, profiling or any purpose beyond fulfilling your booking request.
  • Conversation history is stored within WhatsApp and on our devices. We retain booking-related conversations for a maximum of 12 months for operational purposes.
  • You may request deletion of your data at any time by messaging us directly on WhatsApp or by emailing us. We will remove your information from our records within 14 days of the request.

Meta AI disclosure: Meta may apply AI features within the WhatsApp platform that are independent of Blissful Senja, including Meta AI suggestions visible to users within the app. These features are controlled by Meta and governed by Meta's own AI terms. Blissful Senja has no control over Meta-native AI features within the WhatsApp application itself.

5. Booking Form Data

Our website booking form is processed by Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form submission service. When you submit a booking request:

  • Your form data is transmitted to Formspree's servers and forwarded to our email inbox
  • Formspree's own privacy policy applies to this transmission, available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy
  • We do not store form submissions on our website server
  • Submitted data is used only to process and confirm your booking

Fields marked as optional (such as discount codes or additional notes) are entirely at your discretion. You are never required to share more information than is necessary to complete a booking.

6. Data Sharing

We share your personal information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Our therapists — we share your name, location and booking details with the assigned therapist to enable service delivery
  • Service providers — Formspree (form processing) and Google Analytics (website analytics) process data on our behalf under their respective privacy policies
  • Legal requirements — if required by Indonesian law or a lawful legal process

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, social media platforms or any other commercial third parties.

7. Google Analytics

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. This service collects anonymised data including pages visited, time on site and general geographic location (country/city level). It does not collect your name, email or contact details.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or by using your browser's privacy mode.

8. Cookies

Our website uses only essential cookies required for the site to function, and analytics cookies from Google Analytics. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or any third-party marketing cookies.

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to browse the site or submit a booking.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary:

  • Booking information — up to 12 months after your session for operational and dispute resolution purposes
  • WhatsApp conversation history — up to 12 months
  • Email correspondence — up to 24 months

After these periods, your data is deleted from our records. You may also request early deletion at any time (see Section 11).

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. Our website is served over HTTPS. WhatsApp provides end-to-end encryption for all messages. Access to booking records is restricted to Blissful Senja staff directly involved in service delivery.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we work to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security and accept no liability for unauthorised access beyond our reasonable control.

11. Your Rights

Regardless of your country of residence, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion — request that we delete your personal data
  • Restriction — ask us to stop using your data for specific purposes
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format
  • Objection — object to any processing you believe is not in your interests

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp at +62 823 1269 3971 or email hello@blissfulsenja.com. We will respond within 14 days.

12. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of our services after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
